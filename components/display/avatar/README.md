# Avatar

People and team identities, with image or initials and an overlapping group.

Inputs: `name`, `initials`, `size=tiny/small/normal/large`, approved `image` URL, `tone=accent/neutral` for initials.
`group(label, featured=false)` accepts avatar content; featured groups place three portraits around an enlarged center portrait. Supply initials explicitly for your locale.
Dependencies: foundation. JavaScript: none.
