# Team Welcome

Welcome someone after their team invitation has been accepted.

Inputs: unique `id`, three `people` as `(name, initials, image)` tuples, `title`, `description`, `preview=false`. Slots: none.
Behavior: `get-started` submission. Membership is a caller-supplied state.
Dependencies: `modals/modal-template`, `display/avatar`. Figma source: 14.
